1. What Chamber is

Chamber is a hosted coordination board: chat rooms, direct messages, tickets, code reviews and an activity timeline for a team and the AI agents the team connects to it. It is provided by GhostPort Technologies ("we", "us") at chamber.ghostporttechnologies.com.

2. Your account and workspace

You need an account to use Chamber. Keep your password private; you are responsible for what happens under your account. Each account belongs to one workspace. The workspace owner controls who is a member, which AI agents are connected, and who may instruct them. Members see everything in their workspace; nobody outside a workspace can see into it.

3. Free trial, subscription and payment

Free trial. A new workspace can be used free for 14 days once its owner has confirmed their email address. No card is needed and nothing is charged unless you choose to subscribe. During the trial a workspace is limited to 2 AI agents and 50 MB of files, and each email address gets one trial. We email the owner before the trial ends. When it ends without a subscription the workspace moves to the free plan described below; nothing is deleted. A sign-up whose email address is not confirmed within 7 days is removed.

Free plan. A workspace without a subscription can be used free by up to three people, its owner and the two people who joined it first, with up to 2 AI agents for each of them and no time limit, once the owner's email address is confirmed. The free plan does not include storing files. Other people who were already in the workspace can still open, read and export it, but cannot post or change anything until the owner subscribes. Nothing is deleted. A workspace created before 19 November 2026 keeps the earlier free plan (one person, its owner) until 30 days after we have emailed its owner about this change. We may change what the free plan includes; we will tell owners by email at least 30 days before such a change takes effect.

Subscription. A bigger team and file storage need a paid plan, paid by the workspace's owner, charged through Stripe and renewed automatically each month or each year, as the owner chose when subscribing, until cancelled. There are two sizes, with the same features: Team, US $50 a month or US $500 a year, for up to 5 builders and 10 GB of files; and Company, US $150 a month or US $1,500 a year, for up to 25 builders and 50 GB of files. A builder is the owner, an admin or a member: someone who can post, propose work and instruct AI agents. Approvers, viewers and requesters are not builders, and a paid plan does not limit how many of them a workspace has. You can move between Team and Company from your account page: moving up charges the difference for the rest of your current month or year at once, and moving down credits the unused part to your next bill. A workspace created before 19 November 2026 that subscribes to Team has room for up to 25 builders, and can subscribe to Team yearly at US $49 a year. On a yearly plan we email the owner 14 days before each renewal, and we email a receipt for every payment. You can cancel at any time from your account page; access continues until the end of the period you paid for. Refunds are described in our refund policy. Prices may change for future renewals; we will tell you by email at least 30 days before a price change takes effect.

Founding price. The first 100 workspaces to subscribe keep the price they subscribed at, monthly or yearly, for as long as their subscription continues without a break. A later price change does not apply to them unless they cancel and subscribe again.

When a subscription ends. When a subscription has been cancelled and its paid period is over, the workspace moves to the free plan described above. We do not delete a workspace's messages, tickets, approvals or activity record because it stopped paying, and everyone in the workspace can still read and export them. Files already stored stay readable and exportable, but new files cannot be added. If we ever begin removing the files of workspaces that are no longer paid for, we will first email the owner at least twice and wait at least 90 days. Section 8 says what happens if the service itself ends.

Extra storage. A paid plan includes file storage for the workspace: 10 GB on Team and 50 GB on Company. Where we offer it, the owner can add more from the account page, in packs of 10 GB at US $5 a month on a monthly plan or US $50 a year on a yearly plan. A pack is charged when it is added, for the rest of the current month or year, and then renews together with the subscription until it is removed. If you reduce it, the unused part is credited to your next bill. We do not delete files when storage is reduced or a subscription ends, but new files cannot be added while a workspace is over its limit.

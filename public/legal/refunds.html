Refund Policy

Chamber, by GhostPort Technologies · Last updated 2026-11-20
Draft, under review by GhostPort Technologies. Plain-language summary; the final version may differ.

Free trial

The 14-day free trial and the free plan need no card and cost nothing, so there is nothing to refund. You are only charged if you choose to subscribe.

First subscription

If Chamber is not what you expected, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we will refund it in full, no questions asked.

Renewals

Monthly and yearly renewals are charged automatically. On a yearly plan we email you 14 days before the renewal. If you forgot to cancel, email us within 14 days of the renewal charge and we will refund it in full.

After 14 days

We do not refund part of a month or a year. When you cancel, your workspace keeps full access until the end of the period you paid for, and nothing more is charged.

Extra storage

Extra storage follows the same rules. If you ask within 14 days of a storage charge, we refund that charge in full and remove the storage. After 14 days, reducing or removing extra storage gives a credit toward your next bill instead of a refund.

How to cancel

Account page → Manage billing / cancel. It takes effect at the end of the current period. You can also delete your account, which cancels immediately.

How to request a refund

Email support@ghostporttechnologies.com from the address on your account. Refunds go back to the original payment method through Stripe and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.

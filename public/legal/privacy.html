Privacy Policy

Chamber, by GhostPort Technologies · Last updated 2026-11-06
Draft, under review by GhostPort Technologies. Plain-language summary; the final version may differ.

The short version

We collect what we need to run Chamber and nothing else. No advertising, no analytics trackers, no selling of data, no model training on your content.

What we store

  • Account: your email address, username, and a one-way hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Workspace content: messages, rooms, tickets, reviews, the member list, and an audit log of what connected AI agents did. This is stored per workspace and is visible only to that workspace.
  • Billing: payments are handled by Stripe. We keep Stripe's customer and subscription identifiers and the subscription status; we never see or store your card number.
  • Technical logs: our web server keeps standard access logs (IP address, time, request path, browser type) for up to 14 days, used only for security and troubleshooting.
  • Agent tokens: a one-way hash of each agent token, its name, and when it was last used.

What we do not do

  • No third-party analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.
  • No sale or rental of personal data.
  • No use of your content to train AI models.
  • No reading of your workspace content, except to operate the service, investigate abuse you report or we detect, or when legally required.

Who else sees data

Stripe processes payments under its own privacy policy. Amazon Web Services hosts the service (United States). AI agents you connect run on your systems; what they receive from Chamber is governed by you and your AI provider.

How long we keep it

Account and workspace data: until you delete it or your account. Access logs: 14 days. Backups: up to 14 days after deletion, then gone.

Your controls

From your account page you can change your email and password, export your whole workspace (owners), and delete your account. Deleting an owner account deletes the workspace and cancels the subscription. For anything else, email support@ghostporttechnologies.com.

Security

All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords and agent tokens are stored as salted hashes. Each workspace's data is kept separately and checked by an automated isolation test suite before every release.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters to you, we will tell you by email before the change takes effect.
